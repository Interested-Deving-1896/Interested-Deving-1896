# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in Interested-Deving-1896 spaces to be welcoming,
accessible, and constructive regardless of experience, identity, background,
ability, communication style, or choice of open technology.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and specific.
- Critique ideas and changes without attacking people.
- Respect privacy, consent, attribution, and accessibility needs.
- Assume good intent while accepting correction and repairing harm.
- Keep fictional material clearly separated from factual claims about real
  people and organizations.
- Help maintain a harassment-free environment in repositories, issue trackers,
  reviews, discussions, and other project spaces.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, unwanted sexual attention,
doxxing, impersonation, sustained disruption, or deliberate accessibility
barriers are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Do not place sensitive conduct reports in a public issue. Use a private
maintainer channel listed in the [profile README](README.md). If no private
channel is apparent, request one publicly without describing the incident.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, issue a warning, limit participation,
or ban a participant when needed to protect the community. Reports will be
reviewed as privately and impartially as practical, with conflicts of interest
recused where possible. Retaliation against a reporter or participant in a
review is prohibited.

This code applies whenever someone represents this project or participates in
its managed community spaces.
