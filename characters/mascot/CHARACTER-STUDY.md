# Relay — independent mascot character study

> **Status:** Working title and first-edition character canon.

Relay is the independent mascot of the Mirrorchain ecosystem. Relay represents
the work rather than its maintainer: an approachable companion who follows
changes from their source, checks that each handoff remains faithful, and helps
people find the correct place to contribute.

## Core idea

Relay is a small prismatic digital creature assembled from softly rounded
panels. The panels can separate slightly to reveal paths of light between them,
turning synchronization and provenance into something visible rather than
hidden.

Relay is not a dragon, a person, an AI assistant, or a security authority. The
character is an original visual metaphor for an inspectable open-source relay.

## Personality

- Curious and quick to investigate unfamiliar systems.
- Patient when explaining where a project came from and where changes belong.
- Delighted by reproducible builds, clean histories, and recovered systems.
- Protective of attribution, consent, accessibility, and user control.
- Occasionally distracted by tiny instances of drift and determined to check
  them twice.

Relay solves problems through observation, communication, repair, and clear
handoffs—not secrecy, coercion, or control.

## Recognizable traits

- Two bracket-shaped listening fins that suggest open and close delimiters.
- A split luminous tail whose branches converge into one verified path.
- A three-node chest light representing Source, OSP, and OOC.
- Rounded panel seams that remain visible to communicate inspectability.
- A compact silhouette that works at avatar, badge, and documentation sizes.

## Expressions across the chain

Relay retains the same body and personality in every location. Only the light
accent and carried object change:

| Expression | Accent | Object | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| Source spark | Warm amber | Open notebook and small multitool | Origin, experimentation, and documentation |
| Continuity prism | Cyan-blue | Clear verification prism | Integrity, recovery, and operational continuity |
| Connection constellation | Violet | Star-map ribbon connecting small nodes | Discovery, integration, and ecosystem reach |

## Appropriate uses

- Documentation illustrations and onboarding
- Release, synchronization, recovery, and accessibility themes
- Status dashboards and friendly empty states
- Stickers, badges, social images, and community artwork

Relay must not be used to imply that a mirror is the authoritative source or
that a fictional action actually occurred.
