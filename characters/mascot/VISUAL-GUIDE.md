# Relay visual guide

This guide keeps Relay recognizable while leaving room for different artists
and accessible presentations.

## Required visual anchors

1. A small, non-human, prismatic creature made from rounded panels.
2. Two bracket-shaped listening fins.
3. One split luminous tail that visually reconverges.
4. A three-node chest light.
5. Visible seams and friendly proportions.

Relay must remain visually distinct from KDE's dragon family and from existing
film, television, comic, game, or literary characters.

## Palette roles

Colors identify context but must never be the only identifier.

| Context | Primary accent | Secondary cue |
|---|---|---|
| Source | Amber | Notebook or multitool |
| OSP | Cyan-blue | Verification prism |
| OOC | Violet | Constellation ribbon |
| Whole chain | All three | Connected three-node mark |

Exact color values will be selected after contrast testing. Every color-coded
asset must include a shape, label, texture, or object cue that remains usable
under common color-vision differences and in monochrome.

## Asset requirements

- Provide transparent-background PNG and an editable source where available.
- Include a concise alt-text file or adjacent Markdown description.
- Record creator, date, tool involvement, and license in asset metadata.
- Test recognizable silhouettes at 32, 64, and 128 pixels.
- Avoid embedded text in the base character asset.

## Avoid

- Dragon anatomy, KDE antlers, KDE scarves, or KDE marks
- Weapons, uniforms, intelligence insignia, or government symbols
- Photorealistic surveillance, tactical, or militarized presentation
- Black-box surfaces that hide all seams or provenance cues
- Visual imitation of the fictional characters that informed early ideation
