# Mirrorchain characters

The Mirrorchain character system gives the Interested-Deving-1896, OpenOS
Project OSP, and OpenOS Project Ecosystem OOC relationship a friendly creative
language without replacing the projects' factual documentation.

It has two deliberately separate identities:

| Identity | Role | Relationship to the projects |
|---|---|---|
| **Relay** *(working title)* | Independent mascot | Represents the values and continuity of the ecosystem itself |
| **Nexus, the Mirrorchain Weaver** *(working title)* | Digital cosplay persona | A fictional role portrayed by Interested-Deving-1896 in creative work |

Relay can appear without Nexus in release notes, documentation, accessibility
guides, status pages, and community art. Nexus belongs in character studies,
cosplay concepts, narrative illustrations, and other explicitly creative
contexts. When they appear together, they are companions rather than two
versions of the same character.

## Three layers

Both identities can reflect the three positions in the mirror chain while
remaining recognizable:

| Layer | Relay expression | Nexus costume |
|---|---|---|
| Interested-Deving-1896 | Source spark | Source Weaver |
| OpenOS-Project-OSP | Continuity prism | Mirror Keeper |
| OpenOS-Project-Ecosystem-OOC | Connection constellation | Ecosystem Wayfinder |

## Boundaries

- Character lore never overrides repository documentation or operational data.
- Creative writing must be labelled as fiction wherever context could be lost.
- No character claims affiliation with a government, military, intelligence
  service, corporation, person, or unrelated open-source community.
- Artwork must not copy KDE's dragons, logos, silhouettes, or another fictional
  character's distinctive costume, likeness, symbols, or story.
- Accessibility descriptions and a text equivalent accompany every published
  visual asset.
