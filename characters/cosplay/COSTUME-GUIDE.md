# Nexus digital cosplay costume guide

Nexus has one base silhouette and three costumes. Each costume communicates a
Mirrorchain role without implying hierarchy or authority over contributors.

## Shared base

- Face-neutral luminous visor with no facial likeness
- Layered work coat made from visibly repairable panels
- Three-node clasp corresponding to Source, OSP, and OOC
- Open hands and practical tools rather than weapons
- Repeated bracket and branching-path motifs shared with Relay

## Source Weaver

Represents Interested-Deving-1896 as the experimental source workspace.

- Warm amber seam lighting
- Modular tool roll, notebook, and loose thread-like data paths
- Slightly asymmetrical layers suggesting work in progress
- Pose: building, documenting, or inviting examination

## Mirror Keeper

Represents OSP as an operational continuity layer.

- Cyan-blue seam lighting
- Transparent prism used to compare two visible patterns
- More structured coat panels with repair marks retained
- Pose: verifying, restoring, or handing a copy onward

## Ecosystem Wayfinder

Represents OOC as an outward ecosystem and integration layer.

- Violet seam lighting
- Constellation ribbon and portable map of connected project nodes
- Flexible outer layer suited to travel between communities
- Pose: introducing, mapping, or connecting—with permission

## Cosplay and artwork boundaries

- Do not reproduce a recognizable costume, symbol, prop, or silhouette from an
  existing franchise character.
- Do not use government, military, police, intelligence, or corporate insignia.
- Do not depict unauthorized access, coercion, surveillance, or control as a
  project capability.
- Label public narrative images as fictional character or cosplay artwork.
- Keep practical construction notes separate from fictional lore.
