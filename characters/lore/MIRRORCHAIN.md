# The Mirrorchain — project folklore

> This is fictional project folklore. For actual architecture and contribution
> guidance, use the repository documentation.

The Mirrorchain is imagined as a sequence of open workshops connected by paths
of visible light. Every object travelling along a path carries a tag pointing
back to its workshop of origin. Nothing in the chain becomes more authentic by
being copied; continuity comes from keeping the relationship inspectable.

## The three workshops

### The Source Workshop

The Source Workshop represents Interested-Deving-1896: unfinished benches,
labelled experiments, field notes, recovered parts, and tools designed to work
in more than one environment. This is where changes begin and where questions
about the work return.

### The Continuity Harbor

The Continuity Harbor represents OpenOS-Project-OSP. It receives documented
work, checks its visible pattern, and keeps an operational copy available. The
harbor does not claim authorship over cargo and does not replace its origin.

### The Open Constellation

The Open Constellation represents OpenOS-Project-Ecosystem-OOC. It maps
relationships beyond one workshop, provides additional continuity, and helps
people discover adjacent projects. Every connection remains labelled and can
be followed toward its source.

## Relay and Nexus

Relay travels the entire route, listening for mismatched patterns and carrying
small pieces of context. Nexus follows as a systems artisan, changing costume
to match the kind of work needed at each layer. Their recurring lesson is that
resilience is strongest when origins, transformations, and destinations remain
visible.

## Translation to real systems

| Folklore | Technical meaning |
|---|---|
| Visible path | Documented synchronization direction |
| Origin tag | Upstream attribution and source link |
| Pattern check | Integrity and drift verification |
| Harbor | Operational mirror and recovery endpoint |
| Constellation | Ecosystem discovery and integration |

## The Stewardship Ledger

Resources moving between the workshops are imagined through the
[Stewardship Ledger](STEWARDSHIP-LEDGER.md), a fictional tier system built
around visible purpose, bounded missions, public evidence, and separation of
funding from authority.

The folklore deliberately excludes covert organizations, warfare, criminal
networks, real-world political bodies, and claims of hidden influence.
