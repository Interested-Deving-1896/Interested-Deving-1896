# Support

## Finding the right endpoint

OpenOS-Project-OSP is an operational-continuity namespace, not a universal
support desk for every hosted repository/project. Read the relevant project's
README, provenance notice, and contribution guidance first.

- Ask product or usage questions at the declared source project.
- Report profile, continuity, provenance, or accessibility problems through
  this profile repository's issue tracker.
- Report sensitive security concerns privately according to
  [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- When a copy is identified as a mirror, expect it to be refreshed and avoid
  relying on direct edits unless its guidance explicitly permits them.

These instructions apply across forges; a service may call its collaboration
space a group, organization, workspace, or project.

## Funding

The [OpenOS Project Open Collective](https://opencollective.com/openos-project)
is authoritative for active contribution options, amounts, and financial
terms. Supporting a tier does not buy governance authority or guarantee a
service or outcome.

Community support is best-effort and is not an emergency, paid, legal,
financial, or guaranteed security-response service.
