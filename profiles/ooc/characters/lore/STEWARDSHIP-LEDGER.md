# The Constellation Ledger — OOC fictional folklore

> This is fictional OpenOS-Project-Ecosystem-OOC folklore, not a description of
> real finances, contributors, organizations, or OpenCollective activity. For
> actual contribution options and terms, use the
> [OpenOS Project OpenCollective](https://opencollective.com/openos-project/contribute).

In OOC folklore, the Constellation Ledger maps resources travelling between
independent ecosystem stars. Every entry identifies the relationship it
supports, the public benefit sought, its limits, and the evidence that will
return when the mission is complete.

Relay appears through the **Connection Constellation**, keeping each route
labelled. Nexus appears as the **Ecosystem Wayfinder**, checking that no
contribution silently converts collaboration into control. Neither character
owns the map or grants secret access.

## The four ecosystem circles

The circles are narrative metaphors. They are not current contribution products,
prices, ranks, entitlements, or promised benefits.

### Signal Stars

Small signals of support sustain discovery, documentation, accessibility,
testing, and community context. A Signal Star never encodes a hidden message or
purchases preferential treatment.

### Bridge Rings

Bridge Rings represent recurring support for integrations, shared applications,
portable tooling, contributor resources, and public learning materials. Each
bridge names both endpoints, its maintenance expectations, and a review date.

### Steward Constellations

Steward Constellations represent larger, milestone-bound ecosystem work:
interoperability, accessible participation, open standards, sustainability,
replaceable services, and cross-project infrastructure. Funding remains
separate from technical authority, security access, and governance.

### Open Commissions

An Open Commission is a custom proposal for a bounded public benefit. It names
the objective, eligible costs, decision process, deliverables, risks, evidence,
and completion or retirement conditions before work begins.

It is never a blank cheque, private auction, protection payment, or covert
contract. A proposal involving secrecy, coercion, retaliation, concealed
beneficiaries, or unlawful activity cannot enter the Constellation Ledger.

## From shadow trope to open connection

| Rejected shadow trope | OOC folklore transformation |
|---|---|
| Payment as coded signal | Public purpose, project reference, and readable outcome record |
| Sponsorship as protection | Shared resilience available under common ecosystem rules |
| Hidden institutional payroll | Scoped grants, declared conflicts, milestones, and reporting |
| Custom contribution as auction | Open proposals evaluated against published criteria |
| Financial power as control | Separation of funding, maintainership, governance, and access |
| Permanent obligation | Review dates, stop conditions, and a retired state |

The story's challenge is not whether the Ecosystem Wayfinder can be trusted
personally. It is whether every star remains autonomous, attributable, and free
to replace a route when resources, urgency, and influence grow.

## Constellation-entry template

| Entry | Story function |
|---|---|
| Scope | Names the OOC project relationship or shared ecosystem route |
| Objective | Describes the public ecosystem outcome sought |
| Eligible costs | Defines which integration or participation resources may be supported |
| Allocation | Shows how resources travel without obscuring project autonomy |
| Cadence | Establishes one-time, recurring, or milestone-based support |
| Evidence | Identifies the public outcome record returned by Relay |
| Dependencies | Makes approvals, thresholds, partners, and constraints visible |
| Status | Marks the mission proposed, active, paused, fulfilled, or retired |

## Canon boundaries

- This folklore makes no allegation about any real contribution or contributor.
- It assigns no criminal, intelligence, military, governmental, or covert role
  to OpenOS-Project-Ecosystem-OOC, OpenCollective, or any person.
- It makes no claim about a fiscal host's legal or tax status.
- Live names, amounts, availability, and terms are intentionally excluded.
- Operational documentation, financial records, licenses, and governance rules
  always override fictional language.

The Constellation Ledger makes one OOC principle memorable: an ecosystem is
strongest when purpose, autonomy, attribution, and connection remain visible.
