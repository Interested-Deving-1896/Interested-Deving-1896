# Support and stewardship

The [OpenOS Project public funding page](https://opencollective.com/openos-project)
connects contributions to named purposes. OOC interprets tier families as
ecosystem scopes rather than rank, access, governance authority, or guaranteed
service.

| Tier family | OOC stewardship scope |
|---|---|
| Ecosystem engineering | Distributions, applications, interfaces, integrations, and shared research |
| Discovery and federation | Cross-project initiatives and relationships between independent collectives |
| Contributor and user access | Project support, development equipment, and participation tools |
| Sustainability | Environmental, social, and long-term ecosystem proposals |
| Shared operations | Infrastructure, maintenance, distribution, and logistics coordinated with the operational layer |

Every published tier should identify its purpose, eligible costs, allocation,
cadence, public evidence, dependencies, and current status. The live funding
listing remains authoritative for availability, wording, amounts, fulfillment,
and financial terms.

Fictional character lore is kept separately under `characters/lore/`. It does
not describe real financial, security, governance, or operational activity.
