# Discovery and interoperability

Discoverability is most useful when relationships are explicit and integrations
remain replaceable.

## Minimum discovery record

Each ecosystem entry should identify:

- its authoritative upstream location and maintainers;
- its purpose and current maintenance status;
- its license and attribution requirements;
- supported interfaces, formats, or protocols;
- known dependencies and integration boundaries; and
- where users can find contribution and support guidance.

## Integration principles

- Prefer documented, versioned interfaces over provider-specific coupling.
- Use open formats and exportable data wherever practical.
- Distinguish tested compatibility from a proposed or experimental connection.
- Preserve independent project identity and governance.
- Document how an integration degrades or can be replaced.

## Continuity

Additional copies can improve availability, but only when their provenance and
synchronization status are visible. A stale or transformed copy should never
silently present itself as the authoritative project.
