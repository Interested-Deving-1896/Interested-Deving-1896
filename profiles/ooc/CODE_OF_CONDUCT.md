# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

OpenOS-Project-Ecosystem-OOC aims to provide a welcoming, accessible, and
constructive environment for ecosystem collaboration across communities,
technologies, and Git forges, regardless of experience, identity, background,
ability, communication style, or platform preference.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and specific.
- Critique ideas and changes without attacking people or upstream communities.
- Respect privacy, consent, attribution, licenses, and accessibility needs.
- Describe ecosystem relationships, compatibility, and provenance accurately.
- Keep fictional material clearly separate from factual claims about real
  people, projects, and organizations.
- Help maintain a harassment-free environment in repositories/projects, issue
  trackers, reviews, discussions, and other OOC-managed spaces.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, unwanted sexual attention,
doxxing, impersonation, sustained disruption, misrepresentation of affiliation,
attribution removal, or deliberate accessibility barriers are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Do not place sensitive conduct reports in a public issue. Use a private
maintainer channel listed in the [profile README](README.md). If no private
channel is apparent, request one publicly without describing the incident.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, issue a warning, limit participation,
or ban a participant when needed to protect the community. Reports will be
reviewed as privately and impartially as practical, with conflicts of interest
recused where possible. Retaliation is prohibited.

This code applies whenever someone represents OOC or participates in its
managed community spaces on any forge.
